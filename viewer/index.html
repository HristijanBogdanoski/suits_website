The collection

One pattern, six cloths. Each card is the same garment rendered in that cloth, lit by the same studio light as the hero.

The house

Founded in Naples, 1934. Four cutters, one pattern room, and a waiting list we have never tried to shorten.

A VESTRA suit begins as twenty-two measurements and a standing posture, drawn into a paper pattern that carries your name. That pattern is cut once, corrected twice, and then kept — so the second suit takes eleven weeks, and the tenth takes six.

Everything is full canvas. The chest is floated, not fused, which is why the jacket holds its shape after a decade and why it will take the shape of your shoulders rather than insisting on its own.

  1. 01

    The measure

    Ninety minutes. Twenty-two measurements, posture, and the way you actually stand rather than how you stand when someone is looking.

  2. 02

    The baste

    The first fitting, cut in your cloth and held with white thread. Nothing is finished, so everything can still move.

  3. 03

    The balance

    The fitting that earns the price. Collar, shoulder and drape corrected against the body, not the pattern.

  4. 04

    The finish

    Buttonholes, lining and hems by hand. Your pattern goes into the rack, where it stays for life.
